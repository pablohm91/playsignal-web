How it works

No builds to send. No testers to recruit.

We work from what real players already did on stream.

1

Real sessions, at scale

We gather post-launch streams of your game from independent streamers, including people who played it through to the end.

2

Only what's about your game

We separate real gameplay from chat, other games and in-game text read aloud, then group what players said into recurring findings.

3

Checked by a human

Every quote is reviewed in context before it reaches you, so each finding is something a player really said about your game.

The report

Strengths and weaknesses, side by side

Executive summary

The three things worth doing first, ranked by how many players they affected.

Bugs to fix first

Crashes, soft locks and broken interactions, with the moment each player hit them.

Where players get stuck

Confusing objectives, unclear mechanics and the places where several players got lost.

Recurring complaints

What frustrated more than one player, counted by how many streamers said it.

What players love

The praise that comes up unprompted: useful for your next patch notes and your marketing.

Moments and drop-offs

The scares, laughs and surprises that stuck, how long each streamer played, and why they stopped.

It is especially valuable that both the strengths and weaknesses are analyzed. This is genuinely very useful material.
