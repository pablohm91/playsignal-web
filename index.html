How it works

No builds to send. No testers to recruit.

We work from what real players already did on stream.

1

Whole sessions, start to finish

We follow every streamer through their entire session, including the ones who play to the end. That's where stalls, difficulty spikes and drop-offs show up.

2

Only what's about your game

We separate real gameplay from chat, other games and in-game text read aloud, then group what players said into recurring findings.

3

Checked by a person

Every finding is reviewed in context before it reaches you. What is simply part of your genre's challenge stays out; what your team can act on stays in.
