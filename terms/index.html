Terms & refunds

How ordering, delivery and refunds work.

The service

We find public Twitch broadcasts of your game, follow each streamer's session, and deliver a written playtest report: bugs, sticking points, recurring complaints, praise, how far each streamer got and why they stopped. Every finding is reviewed by a person and linked to the minute of the broadcast it comes from.

The report reflects what streamers said and did on their public broadcasts. It does not replace your own testing, and links work only while the streamer keeps the recording on Twitch.

Free preview

Before you buy, you can ask for a free preview: how much streamed play there is for your game and its three main findings. There is no obligation to buy.

Prices and payment

Snapshot (up to 10 streamers) $149 · Launch report (15–25 streamers) $349 · Publisher pack (three Launch reports) $890. Prices are in US dollars and are paid online by card through Stripe when you order. VAT is applied where the law requires it: businesses in the EU outside Spain with a valid VAT number are invoiced without VAT (reverse charge); customers outside the EU are invoiced without Spanish VAT. You receive an invoice by email.

Delivery

We start as soon as the order arrives and normally deliver the report by email within three working days. Twitch deletes many recordings after 7 to 14 days, so the sooner you order after your launch, the more streams we can include.

Not enough streams

If there isn't enough streamed play to produce the report you ordered, we tell you before we start and refund you in full, or offer the smaller report and refund the difference.

Money-back guarantee

If the report isn't useful to you, email hello@playsignal.games within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund you in full. No questions.

Your rights as a consumer

If you buy as a consumer in the EU, you can withdraw within 14 days. Because the report is a service made for you, once we have delivered it with your agreement the right of withdrawal no longer applies, but the 30-day money-back guarantee above still does.

Liability and law

Our liability is limited to the price you paid for the report. These terms are governed by Spanish law.
