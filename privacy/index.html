Studios and publishers

When you request a preview or order a report, we use your email, your name if you give it, and the game you ask about, to answer you and deliver the work (contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). Forms are processed by Formspree, payments by Stripe (your card details never reach us), and email by Zoho Mail (EU). We keep invoicing records for as long as Spanish tax law requires, and the rest of your request for up to 12 months after delivery.

Streamers

For the games we analyse, we use public Twitch broadcasts: the channel name, the spoken commentary and its transcript with timestamps, and short quotes in the reports. The basis is our legitimate interest in giving game developers feedback on their game (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Audio is transcribed on our own machine and deleted after transcription; transcripts are analysed with the help of an AI service (Anthropic) and kept only as long as needed for the report. Reports go only to the studio or publisher that ordered them; our public sample is anonymised. We never re-upload video or audio, and links always point to the original broadcast. If you're a streamer and want your broadcasts left out, email us with your channel name and we'll remove them from our work within 7 days.

Visitors

This site sets no cookies. We count visits with GoatCounter, which does not track you across sites or store personal data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or ask for a copy, by writing to hello@playsignal.games. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).
