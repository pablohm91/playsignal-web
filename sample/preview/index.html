We followed 15 streamers through their whole sessions of the game. Here is what we found: the two main findings in full, so you can judge the quality, and the headlines of the rest.

Checked by hand: we looked at all 13 moments where a streamer ran into a possible bug, each at its exact minute, and kept 5 of them, which make up the 4 bugs in this report.

The main findings

Fix the weapon switch that left a player stuck on the knife

One streamer got stuck on the knife and couldn't switch back to a gun, even after reloading a save, and stopped playing because of it. Separately, the flashlight switches off every time the game is saved.

  • “not letting me switch back” Streamer A · 1h38m
  • “Why the flashlight turns off every time I save?” Streamer C · 1h47m

The 2 bugs in this finding:

1 streamerCannot switch weapons or switch back
1 streamerFlashlight switches off every time the game is saved

Point players to where keys and items are used

11 of 15 streamers held a key, lever or crest without knowing which door it opens, and several walked in circles looking for named rooms. One ended the stream for the night after failing to find the next key.

  • “It just says, use the family crest, but I have not seen where.” Streamer G · 0h52m
