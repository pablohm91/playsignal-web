If you streamed a game we analysed

What we do with public broadcasts, what we never do, and how to be left out.

What we do

When an indie game launches, we watch public Twitch broadcasts of it and note where players get stuck, what breaks and what they enjoy. The developer gets a report with short quotes, each one linked to the moment in your original broadcast. Your feedback reaches the people who can fix the game.

What we never do

We never re-upload your video or audio, and every link points to your own broadcast on Twitch. Reports go only to the studio or publisher of the game; our public sample has every channel name removed. We don't judge you or your stream: the report is about the game. Audio is deleted right after transcription, and transcripts are deleted once the work on that game is finished.

Leave my channel out

Email hello@playsignal.games with your channel name. Within 7 days we'll remove your broadcasts from our work, including reports we can still update, and we won't use them again. No questions asked.
